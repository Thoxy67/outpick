# Inlet app icon proposals

Hand-written 512×512 SVGs (no external refs, no fonts). Each one has `-512.png` and `-80.png` renders made with `rsvg-convert`. `preview.png` is the contact sheet: 512 px on top, 80 px below.

1. **Tile grid**: a 2×2 grid of source tiles (input, TV, home, media server). The input tile is selected by the violet ring, exactly as the overlay behaves.
2. **Port to screen**: the front profile of an HDMI port, whose signal rises as a violet arrow into a monitor.
3. **Inlet streams**: three violet streams converge through the open side of a rounded basin and collect in a glowing dot. This is the abstract "inlet".
4. **TV + panel**: a TV silhouette with the floating dark source panel at the bottom of its screen. The second tile is ringed in violet.
5. **"i" monogram**: a bold geometric lowercase *i*. Its dot is the violet selection ring.
